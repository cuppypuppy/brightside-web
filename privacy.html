Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Brightside is a read-only news app. We take your privacy seriously and collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

Brightside does not collect any personally identifiable information. The app does not require an account, login, or any personal details to use.

When you react to or share a story, anonymous aggregate counts are stored (e.g. "this story received 10 heart reactions"). These counts are not linked to any individual user or device.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data. We do not require an account or login.

Advertising

Brightside shows ads through Google AdMob to support the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA) and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to personalize them if you allow tracking.

On first launch, iOS will ask whether Brightside can track you across apps and websites. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized. See Google's privacy policy for details on what AdMob collects.

Third-party services

Brightside uses the following third-party services to operate:

Supabase — our backend database and storage provider. News content and anonymous reaction counts are stored here. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Google AdMob — used to show ads in the app. See the Advertising section above and Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
