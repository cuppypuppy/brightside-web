About us

Snickers Studio was founded in honor of our founder's beloved dog, Snickers — a scrappy, joyful companion who made every day a little brighter just by being in it.

In his memory, we build apps and services that add genuine value to everyday life. Nothing bloated, nothing noisy — just thoughtful tools that make mornings better, routines smoother, and the world feel a little more human.
